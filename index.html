THE COURSE CONCEPT

OBE made usable.

Designed to bridge the gap between knowing the terminology and applying OBE to your own course.

01

Understand the framework

Build clarity around outcomes, constructive alignment and the logic behind outcome-based teaching and learning.

02

Design your course

Practice writing measurable COs, using Bloom's Taxonomy and aligning outcomes with learning activities.

03

Map & assess

Explore CO–PO mapping, assessment blueprints, rubrics and evidence of student learning.

04

Calculate & improve

Understand attainment methods, analyse results and identify actions for continuous improvement.

COURSE CREATOR

Dr. L. Krishnaraj

Dr. L. Krishnaraj is a teaching professional, academician and researcher with ten years of experience in higher education. He holds a Ph.D. in English with research interests in computational linguistics, language, technology and interdisciplinary research.

His academic work includes teaching, research, academic quality assurance, accreditation-related coordination and faculty/student development initiatives. He aims to make OBE concepts approachable through clear explanations and practical examples that faculty can adapt to their own classrooms.

The course is developed from his teaching and academic experience, with a focus on practical application rather than theory alone.

BEFORE THE COURSE IS LAUNCHED

Frequently asked questions.

Is this course available now?

Not yet. This website is being used to validate interest and gather feedback before course production and launch.

What is the planned course fee?

The planned fee is ₹649 plus GST. This is a proposed course price and may be subject to platform pricing or promotional offers.

Who is the intended audience?

Faculty members, academic coordinators and educators in higher education who want practical guidance on Outcome-Based Education.

What topics may be covered?

OBE fundamentals, measurable Course Outcomes, Bloom's Taxonomy, CO–PO mapping, assessment design, attainment and continuous improvement.
